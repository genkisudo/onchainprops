Carrot Funding Review

Prop firm on Hyperliquid with 1-Phase and 2-Phase challenges up to $100K, a build-your-own challenge builder, an 80% default split, and USDC payouts you can check on a block explorer.

Payouts verified: Yes Rules onchain: No Payout speed: Usually <24h Split: 80% (90% option) Max account: $100k ($200k soon) Chain: Hyperliquid Max leverage: 5x Token: Yes Country: UAE

On-Chain Transparency

Carrot lists its payouts with wallet addresses and transaction links to Arbiscan and HyperEVMScan, and publishes a transparency page explaining how to check them. Rules are still enforced by Carrot's own systems, not by smart contracts, so we mark Rules onchain as No. Check a few payout transactions yourself before relying on the numbers.

About Carrot Funding

Carrot Funding is a prop firm that moved its evaluation and funded trading infrastructure from gTrade to Hyperliquid in August 2026. It offers an 80% default profit split (60% or 90% selectable), challenge accounts from $5K to $100K with $200K listed as coming soon, and USDC payouts that are usually processed within 24 hours.

Jurisdiction: Carrot Funding is registered in the United Arab Emirates (UAE).

Carrot Funding Challenge Rules

Carrot offers a 1-Phase and a 2-Phase Challenge, and since the October 2026 rule update you can build your own: pick the account size, max loss, daily loss, profit split and an optional payout cap. There is no time limit and no minimum number of trading days.

  • Profit target: 5% then 10% on the 2-Phase Challenge, 10% on the 1-Phase Challenge.
  • Max loss: static, 3%, 6% or 8% of the starting balance depending on the setup.
  • Daily loss: 3% or 4%, resetting daily.
  • Profit split: 60%, 80% (default) or 90%.
  • Payout cap: optional lifetime cap of 10% or 20% of the account size in exchange for a lower fee.
  • Leverage: 5x by default. A Hyperliquid-maximum add-on is listed as coming soon.
  • Bots and AI tools: allowed.

Fees are one-time and the fee is the only amount you can lose. Pay by card (Stripe) or crypto (DePay). Carrot runs frequent promo codes, so check the current price before buying.

Payouts

Request a payout from the dashboard once you are eligible. Carrot usually processes it within 24 hours (up to 30 days if a check is needed) and pays USDC on Arbitrum or HyperEVM. The minimum is 100 USDC. Carrot chooses trade by trade how to execute funded orders (see Hybrid Model Execution below), so payout reliability depends on Carrot's own treasury. We recommend a small first payout to test it.

Hybrid Model Execution

When a funded trader submits an order, Carrot Funding routes it through one of two channels at the firm's sole discretion. The trader's P&L outcome is identical either way — only the settlement path differs. The firm selects the execution method on a trade-by-trade basis.

Funded traders have no control over which method is chosen.
