Real Capital, Real Onchain Fills, 100% of Profit, and Very Little Room to Lose

o2 is a decentralized exchange on the Fuel blockchain that runs a central limit order book in Sway smart contracts. Turbo is its funded-trading product: you pay a one-time fee and trade o2's capital instead of your own. There is no profit split, payouts are claimed permissionlessly, and o2 stresses that trades are "not simulated": every order executes onchain, against real liquidity, with real capital.

Important Context: What o2 Turbo Is

Turbo is not a classic evaluation-first prop firm. It is closer to buying a fixed loss budget on someone else's capital. A Turbo Instant account is a child trading-account contract funded from o2's Turbo pool and controlled by your main o2 account; anything withdrawn from it always returns to that parent. Tier figures (size, premium, loss limit) are published onchain and served by o2's API, and orders whose worst-case fill could cross the loss floor are refused before they are placed. That is why we mark Rules Onchain: Yes.

Two caveats. Turbo orders do not trade on o2's public books: each market has a separate Turbo order book where fills come from o2's house market maker and a set of whitelisted market makers, not from other traders. And Turbo Challenges run on a dedicated challenge network with challenge capital, so the evaluation stage is not on the main chain. We could not find a named operating company or jurisdiction in the docs, so we list the country as TBC.

Turbo Instant funds you on purchase: no evaluation, no deposit, no KYC, no expiry. The premium is paid once in USDC and is not refundable. Turbo Challenges cost far less. You trade at up to 3x leverage, need 9% profit on the account size with every position closed, and must never touch a 6% static max drawdown or a 3% daily drawdown (measured from the start of each UTC day). There is no time limit and no minimum trading days, and passing is automatic. A pass earns a funded Turbo Instant account of the same size at 1x, at no extra cost. Challenges can be paid by card or in USDC, and a wallet can hold only one Turbo account or challenge at a time.

Risk Rules on the Funded Account

The funded account has a single loss limit: 4% of the account size, static. It does not trail profit and does not move when you claim. Profit sits on top of it, so a $20,000 account traded up to $20,800 has $1,600 of room, but claiming that profit brings you back toward the floor. Balance is valued at bid for assets held and ask for assets borrowed to short, with fees counted. Near the floor the account goes close-only; if the floor is reached, positions are closed into the book, anything borrowed is repaid and the account ends. You never owe more than the premium.

Ongoing activity requirements apply, see official docs.

Markets, Leverage and Fees

Turbo trades 13 USDC-quoted spot markets: wBTC, ETH, XAUT, UNI, AAVE, ONDO, LINK, ENA, WLD, FUEL, BNB, HYPE and LIT. All can be bought; only wBTC, ETH and XAUT can be shorted, and that list depends on what the Turbo pool can lend. Funded accounts trade at 1x; challenges at 1x, 2x or 3x. There is no funding rate and no borrow interest. Turbo books charge 0.01% maker and 0.025% taker, and o2 sponsors gas. Market, limit, post-only, fill-or-kill, stop, scale and TP/SL orders are supported, and bots can trade through o2's API and SDKs.

Profit Split and Payouts

There is no profit split: everything above the account size is yours. To get paid you claim profit from the Turbo tab to your main o2 account, then withdraw it to your wallet. Claims have no minimum, no waiting period and no manual review, and you do not need to close the account, but every order and position must be closed first. On o2's own example, a $20,000 Turbo IV bought for $500 and traded to $20,600 pays $600, a net gain of $100 after the premium.

Payout Verification

Because claims are permissionless contract calls and withdrawals are ordinary onchain transfers on Fuel, Turbo payouts should be verifiable in a way most prop firms' are not. o2 also publishes Turbo pool and statistics endpoints in its API. We have not yet received a Turbo payout ourselves, reconciled one on a Fuel explorer, or added o2's pool to our automated payout tracking, so for now we mark Payouts Verified: No. That means "not yet verified by us", not "fails verification", and we expect to upgrade it once we have checked claims onchain.

What Gives Us Pause

The 4% static floor is among the tightest in the directory: a funded account closes if its value falls 4% below the account size, and the floor does not rise with profit. Fills come from o2's house and whitelisted market makers on a separate Turbo book, so liquidity and execution quality depend on o2's chosen counterparties. Only three markets can be shorted. Account sizes top out at $20,000, and the premium is a sunk cost, so Turbo Instant only pays off once you make more than the premium. The operator entity is not named in the docs, and Fuel is a smaller ecosystem than Hyperliquid or Arbitrum.

The Verdict

o2 Turbo is one of the most genuinely onchain products we list: real capital, onchain order books, a 100% split, permissionless claims, no KYC and audited contracts. The trade-off is very little room to be wrong under the 4% static floor. It suits disciplined spot traders and bots who can make steady small gains with tight stops. If you try it, start with a $20–$60 challenge rather than a premium account, claim profit often, and check your first claim and withdrawal on a Fuel explorer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is o2 Turbo an onchain prop firm?

Mostly, yes. o2 is a spot exchange on the Fuel blockchain whose order books and trading accounts are Sway smart contracts. A Turbo Instant account is its own onchain contract funded from o2's Turbo pool, its tiers are published onchain, and its trades settle onchain on o2's Turbo order books. Turbo Challenges are the exception: they run on a dedicated challenge network with challenge capital until you pass.

How do o2 Turbo payouts work?

There is no profit split: you keep 100% of everything above the account size. Once all orders and positions are closed you can claim profit from the Turbo tab to your main o2 account at any time, with no minimum, no waiting period and no manual review, and then withdraw it to your wallet like any other balance. The loss floor does not move when you claim.

What are the o2 Turbo drawdown rules?

A funded Turbo Instant account closes if its value falls 4% below the account size; the floor is static and does not rise with profit. Turbo Challenges use a 6% static max drawdown and a 3% daily drawdown measured from the start of each UTC day, and touching either ends the challenge.

What does o2 Turbo cost?

Turbo Instant accounts cost a one-time premium of $75 ($2,500), $150 ($5,000), $250 ($10,000) or $500 ($20,000). Turbo Challenges cost $20, $30, $60 or $120 for the same sizes and, if passed, give a funded Turbo Instant account of that size at no extra cost. Neither is refundable, and there is no deposit or KYC.

What keeps an o2 Turbo account open?

Turbo Instant accounts have no expiry, but they must stay above the 4% loss floor. Ongoing activity requirements apply, see official docs.
