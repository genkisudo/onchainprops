A Kraken-Owned Crypto Prop Firm with Static Drawdowns and USDC Payouts

Important Context: Breakout Is Not an Onchain Prop Firm

Breakout runs its evaluations and funded accounts in a simulated trading environment. Prices and liquidity come from third-party brokers, market makers and exchanges, and Breakout's own FAQ says no real trades are placed. Rules are enforced by Breakout's systems, not smart contracts, which is why it sits in our Crypto Prop Firms section rather than the main Onchain Prop Firms table. The one onchain step is the payout, which arrives as USDC on Ethereum.

Evaluation Types

Every current Breakout evaluation is a single step. The three 1-Step variants share a 3% daily loss limit, which resets at 00:30 UTC based on your balance at that moment. They differ in the upgrade (profit) target and the maximum drawdown. That drawdown is static: it stays fixed at a percentage of your starting balance and does not trail your profits.

Spark is a separate, newer product focused on index and commodity markets, with its own fees and leverage: up to 40x on the S&P 500 and XYZ100 and up to 20x on commodities. It drops the daily loss limit, minimum trading days and consistency rule, but uses a tighter trailing drawdown that follows your highest closed balance, in both the evaluation and the funded account. Traders on the older 2-Step evaluation keep their original rules, including a 4% daily loss limit.

There is no minimum or maximum time limit. You pass as soon as you hit the target without breaking a rule. News trading is allowed, and evaluation fees are non-refundable. Breakout's help center does not list prices. Kraken's Breakout page shows fees from about $20 for a $5K account up to $1,090 for $200K, depending on the plan, so check checkout for current pricing.

Leverage, Markets and Fees

On the 1-Step evaluations, leverage is set per instrument: 10x on BTC, 5x on ETH, SOL, XRP and other majors, 3x on names like HYPE and PEPE, and 2x on smaller altcoins. Non-crypto markets include the S&P 500, the XYZ100 index and copper at 10x, gold, silver and oil at 5x, and natural gas at 3x.

Simulated trading fees are 0.04% per order on crypto and 0.02% on non-crypto assets, plus a 0.033% daily swap on positions held through 00:00 UTC. These fees are notional: they count against your evaluation P&L, but you never pay them.

Profit Split and Allocation

The base split is 80%, and you can pay more at checkout for 90%. Breakout's help center does not publish the split, so confirm it before you buy. You can run several funded accounts at once, up to a combined $200,000 allocation.

Payouts

You can request a payout at any time, weekends included, as long as you have no open positions and the account is not breached. The minimum is $50 after the split. The amount is deducted immediately, and you can keep trading the same account.

Payward Oceanic, Ltd (POL) reviews each request, stating 12–24 hours, often faster. POL then sends USDC on the ERC-20 network to the wallet you enter on your dashboard. USDC is currently the only payout method. Breakout states plainly that payouts are not insured or guaranteed by any institution.

Payouts land as onchain USDC transfers, but Breakout does not publish the sending addresses, so we cannot tie them back to the firm. We have not independently verified payouts and mark Payouts Verified: No in the comparison table.

Company Structure

Kraken acquired Breakout in 2025, and Breakout's mechanics now also power Kraken's in-exchange prop program. Two separate companies are involved. Breakout Trading Group, LLC runs the evaluations and the simulated environment. Once you pass, you complete KYC and sign a Funded Trader Agreement with Payward Oceanic, Ltd (POL), which provides funded accounts and sends payouts. Payward is Kraken's parent company. Approval to trade funded usually takes 12–24 hours.

Breakout is unavailable to residents of sanctioned and high-risk jurisdictions, including Russia, Belarus, Iran, North Korea, Cuba, Syria, Venezuela, and occupied regions of Ukraine. The full list is in the FAQ. You may trade with a VPN, but not to hide your jurisdiction, and KYC must be completed with the VPN switched off.

What Gives Us Pause

A funded account here is a contract on simulated performance, so there is no onchain rulebook and no public payout trail. Payouts depend on POL's review and treasury, and they are explicitly uninsured. Kraken's ownership adds real institutional weight compared with most crypto prop firms, but it is not the same as a guarantee. Fees are non-refundable, and crypto leverage is low compared with onchain perp firms, especially on altcoins.

The Verdict

Breakout is the most institutionally backed option in our crypto prop firm list. It offers static drawdowns, no time limit, payouts on request, and a parent company that runs one of the largest exchanges. If onchain enforcement and verifiable payouts are non-negotiable, stick with the firms in our main table. If you want a centralized evaluation from a well-known name, with stablecoin payouts, it is a strong place to start.
