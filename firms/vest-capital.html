Funded Accounts on a Perp Exchange, with a Single Static Drawdown and Fast USDC Claims

Vest describes the program like this: "Access instant funded accounts, trade stocks and equities around the clock, and manage risk with institutional-grade tools." In practice, crypto trades 24/7, while stocks, indices and commodities lock for the weekend (see the rules below).

Important Context: What Vest Capital Is

Vest Capital is the funded-trading program of Vest Markets, a perpetuals exchange offering crypto, US stocks, ETFs, indices, FX and commodities. You pay a one-time fee, Vest provides the trading capital, and you keep a share of the profit you claim. Funded Accounts cannot receive deposits, so you never put your own money at risk beyond the fee.

It is not a fully onchain prop firm. Evaluation accounts trade simulated capital against live market conditions, and the drawdown and daily-loss rules are enforced by Vest's systems, not by smart contracts. The onchain part is the money rails: you can sign in with a crypto wallet, and profit moves as USDC. That is why we mark Rules Onchain: No in the comparison table.

Account Types

There are three ways in. Evaluation accounts come in Silver ($5K), Gold ($10K) and Platinum ($25K) tiers. Instant Accounts skip the test entirely and are live the moment payment clears.

Drawdown and Loss Rules

Max drawdown is static. It is set when the account is created and never moves: it does not trail profits and it is not recalculated at end of day. On evaluation accounts it is 6% of starting capital, so a Platinum $25,000 account closes if total value, including unrealized PnL, touches $23,500. Instant Accounts use 2% on the $500 size and 4% on the rest.

The optional daily loss limit (3% or 4%) is measured against your equity at 20:00 ET each day. It carries over once the account is Live. Instant Accounts and the 1-Step "no daily loss" option have drawdown only.

A breach of either rule closes the account immediately and permanently, with no warning and no grace period. Fees are simulated during the evaluation but still count toward your balance, so they eat into your drawdown the same way a realized loss would.

Trading Rules, Leverage and Markets

Vest is unusually permissive on strategy. There is no consistency rule, no news-trading restriction, and scalping and high-frequency approaches are allowed. You can hold through weekends. The only prohibition is abusing or coordinating across Funded Accounts, for example taking opposite sides of the same market on two accounts.

Funded Accounts support leverage of up to 75x, depending on the market, with cross margin across equities, indices, crypto, commodities and FX. Vest's docs and FAQ quote slightly different ceilings (75x and 50x), so check the market page for the exact figure. Maker fees are 0 bps; taker fees are 1 bp on most markets and 0.25 bps on indices and commodities. Non-crypto markets lock from Friday 8 PM ET until the weekend reopen.

Restrictions: XRP and HYPE are unavailable on Funded Accounts, and API trading is not supported in the evaluation or on Live accounts. That rules out bots, which matters if you trade algorithmically.

Profit Split and Payouts

The split is 80% or 90% on evaluation accounts and a flat 95% on Instant Accounts. It is fixed at purchase and applied flat at every claim, with no further fees or deductions.

Getting paid takes two steps. First, you submit Claim Profit from the Live Funded Account, and your share is credited to your Primary Account within 24 hours. You may need to close open positions first. Second, you withdraw USDC from the Primary Account to an external wallet on a supported chain. There is no cap on how often you can claim and no minimum to claim, and claims cannot be reversed once started. Withdrawals from the Primary Account process instantly up to $50,000 in a rolling 24 hours.

You can hold up to 10 Live Funded Accounts at once, and up to 100 in the evaluation phase. Vest also offers a Payout Balance payment option that lets you buy new accounts with claimed profit.

Payout Verification

Claims settle as USDC, but Vest does not publish a payout wallet or a public ledger tying individual claims back to the program, and we have not independently verified payouts. We mark Payouts Verified: No in the comparison table. Vest does issue an "Official Payout Certificate" card to traders, which is marketing rather than proof. Treat the 24-hour claim time as the firm's stated policy and test it with a small first claim.

What Gives Us Pause

Rules are enforced by Vest, not onchain, and you rely on the exchange's own risk systems and treasury to honor claims. We could not find a named legal entity or jurisdiction for the program in Vest's public docs, so we list the country as TBC. A breach is instant and unrecoverable, and the cheapest 20%-target accounts need a large win before you earn anything. Account sizes are small compared with other firms, topping out at $25,000. Fees are not refundable after you place a trade, and failing the evaluation never qualifies for a refund.

The Verdict

Vest Capital is a good fit for traders who want simple rules: one static drawdown, no consistency rule, no minimum days and a high split, with very cheap entry through Instant Accounts from $10. If payout verifiability and onchain rule enforcement come first, put the firms at the top of our table ahead of it. If you want a low-cost, low-friction funded account on a real perp exchange, it is worth a small first account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Vest Capital an onchain prop firm?

Only partly. Vest Capital is the funded-account program of Vest Markets, a perpetuals exchange funded with USDC on Base and Arbitrum. Evaluation accounts trade simulated capital, and the risk rules are enforced by Vest's own systems rather than smart contracts. Deposits, profit claims and withdrawals settle in USDC.

How do Vest Capital payouts work?

Payouts are a two-step flow. You submit Claim Profit from a Live Funded Account, and your share is credited to your Primary Account within 24 hours. You then withdraw USDC to an external wallet on a supported chain. There is no cap on how often you can claim, and a claim cannot be reversed once started.

What is Vest Capital's profit split?

Evaluation accounts pay 80% or 90%, chosen at purchase for a higher fee. Instant Accounts pay a flat 95%. The split is fixed when you buy and does not change when the account goes Live.

What are the Vest Capital drawdown and daily loss rules?

Max drawdown is static: 6% of starting capital on evaluation accounts and 2-4% on Instant Accounts. It never trails profits. Evaluation accounts can add a 3% or 4% daily loss limit, or none, and Instant Accounts have no daily limit. Any breach closes the account immediately and permanently.

Does Vest Capital offer refunds?

Yes, within 3 days of purchase, provided no trades have been placed on the account. Failing an evaluation does not qualify for a refund.
