FUNDED by FOXIFY

Instant funding from a $100 deposit, scaling to $20K with no evaluation phase, an 80% manual or 70% automation split, up to 100x leverage and instant USDC payouts.

Payouts verified: Not yet Rules onchain: No Payout speed: Instant Split: 80% (70% automation) Max account: $20,000 Chain: Hyperliquid / Arbitrum / Berachain Max leverage: 100x Token: Yes Country: BVI

What Is FUNDED by FOXIFY?

FUNDED by FOXIFY (powered by Foxify Trade) is a deposit-based funded-trading product. You deposit USDC collateral from $100, receive an instantly funded account with no evaluation phase, and scale up to $20,000 of funded capital as you hit points and profit targets. Foxify says trades route to real DEX orderbooks instead of demo accounts, and that your deposit is the first thing exposed to losses: losses come out of your collateral before the FUNDED vault covers the rest. FUNDED by FOXIFY has its own token, FOX, which has staking.

Jurisdiction: FUNDED by FOXIFY is registered in the British Virgin Islands (BVI).

Platforms

Foxify Automation, a hands-free option that follows curated strategies and is in beta, is live on Hyperliquid, Decibel, Ondo, GMX and KuCoin. Manually traded FUNDED accounts are live on Kodiak Finance (Berachain), what.exchange (Arbitrum) and PERPTools (Arbitrum). Check which venue you are buying on before depositing, because the chain and the USDC variant differ by platform.

Profit Split

Manually traded accounts keep 80% of profits. Foxify Automation accounts keep 70%. Earlier versions of this page listed a 90% Flash Funding path, which does not appear in Foxify's current documentation.

Account Tracks

  • Entry: $100 deposit, $500 starting funded capital, scaling through Spark, Surge, Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum to $10,000. Diamond ($15,000) is coming soon. Max drawdown runs from 20% at Spark down to 10% at Platinum.
  • Pro: $500 deposit, $2,500 starting funded capital, scaling through Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum to $10,000. Max drawdown runs from 20% at Bronze down to 12% at Platinum.

Max funded capital on both tracks is $20,000. Promotion needs 100 points plus the level's profit target and a minimum P&L of +15% of the original balance, and there is no minimum trading time. A drawdown reset is available for a fee, and a dynamic borrow fee accrues if an account sits inactive for a long time.

Trading Conditions

Leverage is up to 100x on crypto perpetuals, 20x on commodities, indices and forex, and 10x on stocks, across 125+ markets. There is no consistency rule, no profit cap, and no KYC. News trading, weekend holding, bots, EAs and copy trading are allowed. Max drawdown is the only rule that can end a challenge. Trading fees are 7.5 bps on most platforms and 9 bps on PERPTools.

Payouts

Once the points and profit targets are met, you request a payout and Foxify settles USDC on-chain with no manual review. Foxify also publishes a $50,000 guarantee if it ever denies a valid payout without cause. Payout transactions are on-chain, but we have not independently verified Foxify payouts, so we mark Payouts verified as No. Start with the smallest track and test a first payout.
