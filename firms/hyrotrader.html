Important Context: HyroTrader Is Not an Onchain Prop Firm

HyroTrader states plainly that all trading takes place on simulated (demo) accounts running on real exchange market data. It is not a broker, accepts no trading deposits, and earns its revenue from challenge fees. Evaluation rules are enforced by the firm's own systems, not smart contracts, which is why it sits in our Crypto Prop Firms section rather than the main Onchain Prop Firms table. Payouts, however, are made in USDT or USDC.

Trading periods have no deadline and a stop loss is not required. Accounts close after 30 days of inactivity. Low-cap altcoins (market cap under $100M) are restricted to under 5% of the initial balance, leverage included. Account sizes are chosen at checkout, up to $200,000 funded.

Profit Split

The base split is 80% on every product. On One-Step and Two-Step it can rise to 90% over time; Zero-Step stays at 80%.

Platforms and Leverage

You can trade on HyroTrader's own platform (700+ USDT perpetual pairs, TradingView charts), or connect an API-linked demo account on Bybit (700+ pairs, up to 150x on BTC and ETH) or BloFin (80+ USDT-M pairs, up to 150x on BTC and ETH). The Tealstreet and Tiger terminals run on Bybit data and are unavailable to US residents and Bybit-restricted countries; HyroTrader says not to bypass this with a VPN.

Payouts

HyroTrader says payouts are processed within 24 hours in USDT or USDC with no fees. The minimum is $100 in profit after the split, and positions must be closed before you request one. On One-Step and Two-Step the refundable deposit returns with your first payout. The site does not name the payout network, so confirm it before requesting.

The firm claims over $8M paid to 1.7K+ funded traders, but we have not independently verified payouts, so we mark Payouts Verified: No in the comparison table.

Company Structure

The platform is operated by HYRO TECHNOLOGIES FZ-LLC (Ras Al Khaimah, UAE). Funded accounts are provided by HYROTRADER TECHNOLOGIES LTD (Tortola, BVI), and card payments for EEA/UK customers go through Hyro Finance, j.s.a. (Bratislava, Slovakia). The team started building in 2022 and launched in 2023.

What Gives Us Pause

A funded account here is a contractual share of simulated performance, so there is no onchain-verifiable rulebook or payout trail. Mainly, payouts depend on the firm's own treasury and good faith. Read the terms in the HyroTrader FAQ before buying, and note the Zero-Step fee cannot be refunded.

The Verdict

HyroTrader is a fairly established centralized option (live since 2023) with cheap entry prices, a no-deadline evaluation, and a clear 24-hour stablecoin payout promise. If onchain enforcement and verifiable payouts are non-negotiable, stick with the firms in our main table. If you want a Bybit or BloFin-style futures workflow, it is worth a look.

Is HyroTrader an onchain prop firm?
