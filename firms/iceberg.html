Iceberg

Instant-funded trading platform powered by Hyperliquid — 80% profit split, 3% daily loss limit and 6% static max loss.

Split: 80% Max Account: $100,000 Chain: Hyperliquid Payouts verified: Not yet

Iceberg offers simulated trading capital on Hyperliquid, with account sizes from $5,000 to $100,000. Traders can choose Instant Funding (no evaluation) or the 1-Step challenge.

Rules

Profit split is 80%. The daily loss limit is 3% and the maximum loss is a 6% static limit. The 1-Step challenge has a 10% profit target; Instant Funding has none. Overnight and weekend holding are allowed.

Payouts

Instant Funding uses a 5-day payout window; the 1-Step challenge offers on-demand payouts after the evaluation. According to Iceberg, account fees fund a public payout pool. We have not yet independently verified Iceberg's payouts onchain, so treat payout claims as unconfirmed until we do.
