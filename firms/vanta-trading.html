Vanta Trading

Onchain prop firm built on Bittensor Subnet 8, with up to $100K funded accounts and an optional Hyperliquid-native mode.

Split: Up to 100% Max Account: $100,000 Chain: Bittensor / Hyperliquid Token: No Country: Cayman Islands

Vanta Trading is a prop firm built by Taoshi, the team behind Bittensor Subnet 8. Trading occurs in a simulated environment that mirrors real market prices — orders do not touch live order books by default. A separate Hyperliquid-native mode is available for eligible traders. The reward split is selectable by the trader, from 60/40 to 100/0. Rewards are paid weekly: crypto rewards settle as USDC on Base with public transaction hashes, while bank rewards go via Stripe in USD and cannot be verified onchain. Accounts are fully simulated.

Account Sizes and Scaling

Accounts run from $5,000 (the minimum) to $100,000. Pro accounts are awarded by automatic promotion when results qualify, at no additional cost, up to $1,000,000 in demo capital.

Pricing and Profit Split

You pay one participation fee, once. The price depends on account size, profit split and daily loss limit. It starts at $33, and the default $100,000 account at 100/0 with a 3% daily limit costs $1,099. A lower split is priced lower.

Splits on offer are 60/40, 70/30, 80/20, 90/10 and 100/0. Size and split are locked for the life of the account. You can hold up to three accounts: one $50K or $100K Classic, one $5K, $10K or $25K Classic, and one Instant Funded.

Payment is by crypto (via NowPayments), card or bank transfer (via Stripe). Prices for every option are on Vanta's verification page; sale prices may change.

How Vanta Trading Payouts Work

Rewards are paid weekly on your scaled account's simulated realized profit, at your split. A payout never reduces your equity or moves your loss limits. Eligible profit is netted against prior losses and payouts, so the same profit is never paid twice.

Rewards arrive in USDC or by bank transfer. Every payout is listed on the public Payout Ledger, and USDC payouts can be checked onchain. KYC happens once, when your scaled account activates.

From Classic to a $1M Pro Account

Vanta has four stages:

  • Classic: pass the 10% one-step evaluation.
  • Earn: your scaled account pays weekly rewards from the day you pass.
  • Grow: automatic promotion to 3x, 5x or 10x your account size. Rewards double during Grow. It lasts at least 90 days and needs a 6% target with a Calmar ratio of 1.75 or more.
  • Pro: up to $1,000,000 in demo capital, with your split unchanged.

There are two promotion tracks. The fast track promotes you at 3x once your scaled account is up 10%. The steady track needs 60+ days, 3%+ profit, a Calmar ratio of 1.5+ and no single day above 50% of total return; it promotes at 5x, or 10x with a Calmar of 2.0+.

Pro rules are stricter: a 5% daily and 8% end-of-day trailing loss limit, plus consistency and martingale rules, on a liquidity-reviewed list of markets. Fail Pro and you return through Classic, then skip Grow on your next pass.

Instant Funded Accounts

Instant Funded skips the evaluation. Accounts are $25,000, $50,000 or $100,000 at an 80/20 split, from $750. They carry a 3% daily loss limit and a 5% or 8% trailing limit. With the optional Pro challenge, you can move to a Pro account of 2.5x, 5x or 10x size. For now it is capped at 50 traders.

Profit Targets

The profit target is 10% on every Classic account (Instant Funded accounts skip the evaluation).

Vanta Rules

  • Profit target: 10% on every Classic account; Instant Funded has no evaluation.
  • Reward split: selectable by the trader, from 60/40 to 100/0.
  • Daily loss limit: selectable, 3% or 5%. The max loss limit remains 5% static.
  • Payouts: rewards are paid weekly. Crypto rewards settle as USDC on Base with public transaction hashes; bank rewards go via Stripe. The public ledger is live.

Business Model

Jurisdiction: Cayman Islands.
