Vanta Trading

Prop firm built on Bittensor Subnet 8, with $5K to $100K one-step evaluations, a split of up to 100%, weekly rewards, and automatic promotion to Pro accounts of up to $1M in demo capital.

Payouts verified: In progress Rules onchain: No Payout speed: Weekly Split: Up to 100% Max account: $100,000 Chain: Bittensor / Hyperliquid Token: No Country: Cayman Islands

Terms last verified Nov 17, 2026

Vanta Trading is a prop firm built by Taoshi, the team behind Bittensor Subnet 8 (Vanta Network), operated by Taoshi VT Services in the Cayman Islands. Trading happens on simulated accounts on the in-house Vanta Trading Desk, priced from Hyperliquid (crypto, commodities, indices and equity futures), Databento and Massive (spot equities and forex). Vanta says its business is copy trading its best traders with live capital rather than earning on failed evaluations. The reward split is selectable by the trader, from 60/40 to 100/0. Rewards are paid weekly in USDC on Base with public transaction hashes, or in Vanta Network alpha. As of Nov 17, 2026, Vanta reports $1.17M in rewards paid all-time.

Track Subnet 8 on third-party explorers: Taostats (main explorer page), Bittensor.ai (subnet overview), TaoMarketCap (market data and trading), Subnet Metrics (burns, emissions and more) and SubnetRadar.

Account Sizes and Scaling

Accounts run from $5,000 (the minimum) to $100,000. Pro accounts are awarded by automatic promotion when results qualify, at no additional cost, up to $1,000,000 in demo capital.

Pricing and Profit Split

The default profit split is 100% to the trader. You can customize the account size, the daily loss limit and the rewards split.

You pay one participation fee, once. The price depends on account size, profit split and daily loss limit. It starts at $33, and the default $100,000 account at 100/0 with a 3% daily limit costs $1,099. A lower split is priced lower.

Splits on offer are 60/40, 70/30, 80/20, 90/10 and 100/0. Size and split are locked for the life of the account. You can hold as many Classic accounts as you like (each must run its own strategy, with no mirroring or opposing positions) plus one Instant Funded account.

Payment is by card (via Stripe) or crypto (via NowPayments). Prices for every option are on Vanta's pricing page; sale prices may change (a 25% FALL discount runs until Nov 25, 2026).

How Vanta Trading Payouts Work

Rewards are paid weekly on your scaled account's simulated realized profit, at your split. A payout never reduces your equity or moves your loss limits. Eligible profit is netted against prior losses and payouts, so the same profit is never paid twice.

Rewards now arrive in USDC or Vanta Network alpha; there is no minimum, allowance or extraction limit. Every payout is listed on the public Payout Ledger, and USDC payouts can be checked onchain on Base. Some older ledger entries were bank transfers via Stripe, which cannot be verified onchain. KYC (through Stripe) happens once, when your scaled account activates.

From Classic to a $1M Pro Account

Vanta has four stages:

  • Classic: pass the 10% one-step evaluation.
  • Earn: your scaled account pays weekly rewards from the day you pass.
  • Grow: automatic promotion to 3x, 5x or 10x your account size. Weekly rewards are your Grow return applied to your Classic size, multiplied by 1.3x (fast track), 1.5x (steady track, 5x) or 2x (steady track, 10x). Grow lasts at least 90 days and needs a 6% target with a Calmar ratio of 1.75 or more.
  • Pro: up to $1,000,000 in demo capital, with your split unchanged.

There are two promotion tracks. The fast track promotes you at 3x if your scaled account is up 3% in the first week after your first trade, 5% in the second week, or 10% after that. The steady track needs 60+ days, 3%+ profit, a Calmar ratio of 1.5+ and no single day above 50% of total return; it promotes at 5x, or 10x with a Calmar of 2.0+. Accounts opened before November 14, 2026 keep the old terms: a 10% fast-track target and 2x Grow rewards.

Pro rules are stricter: a 5% daily and 8% end-of-day trailing loss limit, plus consistency and martingale rules, on a liquidity-reviewed list of markets. Fail Pro and you return through Classic, then skip Grow on your next pass.

Instant Funded Accounts

Instant Funded skips the evaluation. Accounts are $25,000, $50,000 or $100,000 at a fixed 80/20 split, from $750, with weekly rewards from the day you register. They carry a 3% daily loss limit and a 5% or 8% trailing limit from the end-of-day high-water mark. With the optional Pro challenge, you can move to a Pro account of 2.5x, 5x or 10x size. There is no longer a cap on the number of Instant Funded traders, but each person can hold one.

Profit Targets

The profit target is 10% on every Classic account (Instant Funded accounts skip the evaluation).

Vanta Rules

  • Profit target: 10% on every Classic account; Instant Funded has no evaluation.
  • Reward split: selectable by the trader, from 60/40 to 100/0.
  • Daily loss limit: selectable, 3% or 5%. The max loss limit remains 5% static. Trade at least once every 60 days or the account closes.
  • Payouts: rewards are paid weekly in USDC on Base (public transaction hashes) or Vanta Network alpha. The public ledger is live.
