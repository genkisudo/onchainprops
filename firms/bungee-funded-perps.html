A Cheap, One-Step Perps Challenge with a Public Payout Ledger, Strict Trading Rules and a Young Track Record

Bungee's pitch is "Pay once. Get funded. Request payouts on your schedule." You buy a one-step challenge, hit the profit target without touching the daily loss or drawdown limit, and trade a funded account on the same terms, keeping 80% of what you claim.

Important Context: What Bungee Funded Perps Is

Bungee Funded Perps is the prop trading program of Bungee, built by the team behind Socket, the cross-chain bridging and routing infrastructure. The headline figures on the landing page ($40B+ in volume, $15M+ raised) appear to describe Bungee's wider platform rather than the funded program, which is new: its rulebook was first published in September 2026.

It is not a fully onchain prop firm. Accounts are records in Bungee's own ledger, and the rulebook says Bungee may "simulate trades internally, hedge some or all exposure, net exposure across accounts, externally execute trades" at its discretion, and that "the Bungee dashboard and internal account ledger are the authoritative source of truth". What is onchain is the money: you pay in USDC and payouts go out as USDC transfers on Base. That is why we mark Rules Onchain: No, and we have not independently verified payouts, so Payouts Verified: No.

Account Types and Pricing

There are three standard one-step tracks, each in four sizes. All three share a 3% daily loss limit, an 80% split, no time limit, no minimum trading days and no consistency rule. They differ in how much profit you need and how much drawdown you get. The $5K and $200K sizes were shown as "coming soon" when we checked.

Prices come from Bungee's public products API and checkout page and can change, so confirm on the live page before you buy.

One Shot Black is a separate $10,000 account, priced at $24 in the products API when we checked. It has a 10% profit target, no daily drawdown limit, and a trailing maximum drawdown with a fixed $200 allowance (2% of the account). The floor starts at $9,800 and resets at 00:00 UTC each day to equity at that moment minus $200, so it can move up or down at the reset but not intraday. It has its own $30,000 per-trader allocation cap, separate from the standard accounts. The rulebook added it on 26 November 2026, so it is brand new, and we have not confirmed how many accounts are available.

Drawdown and Loss Rules

Bungee uses a hard-breach model. Touch a limit and the account is done: open positions may be force-closed, the account may be permanently disabled, and you buy a new challenge to continue. There is no reset, grace period or soft breach, and dashboard warnings do not change the limits.

Maximum drawdown on Classic, Pro and Turbo is static. It is measured from the starting balance and never trails profits, so a $10,000 Turbo account has a floor at $9,700 and a $10,000 Classic account at $9,400. Risk equity includes unrealized P&L, fees and funding, and breaches are checked on mark prices.

The daily loss limit is 3% of your settled balance at the 00:00 UTC reset, not of the original account size. Settled balance excludes open-position P&L, while the breach check runs against live equity that includes it. The rulebook is explicit that the check also runs at the reset itself: "carrying an open loss across midnight does not exempt it from breach". Unrealized profit at reset does not raise the next day's floor. You have to stay above both the daily floor and the static drawdown floor, and on a Turbo that has lost money the 3% static floor is usually the tighter one.

Trading Rules, Leverage and Markets

Bungee lists 31 markets across crypto (19), stocks (7), indices (2) and commodities (3), and a market must be approved by Bungee before it can be traded. All are isolated-margin perpetuals; cross margin is not supported.

Leverage is fixed per market and not adjustable: 20x on the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100, 10x on BTC and gold, 5x on ETH and silver, 3x on SOL, HYPE, XRP, BNB and a few others, and 2x on most remaining altcoins, crude oil and most listed stocks. Each market also has a maximum position notional, up to $1,000,000 on the largest. The landing page says "up to 5x" while the markets page lists 20x on indices, so use the markets page as the source of truth. When Bungee changes a market's leverage, existing positions keep their old leverage and become reduce-only.

Fees are 1.5 basis points (0.015%) of executed notional on open and close, with maker and taker charged the same. Funding is charged hourly on open notional, with a floor of 0% and no credits. Funding is never negative, so you pay it or pay nothing, whichever side you are on. Limit orders fill against eligible executed Hyperliquid trades and TP/SL triggers use executed Hyperliquid trade prices, while market orders use Bungee's order-book snapshots with modeled spread and slippage.

What you cannot do:

  • Bots, APIs, HFT and scalping are banned on challenge and funded accounts. Scalping is judged on the pattern, with no published holding time or trade count, and "a small number of rapid trades in one session can be enough". Confirmed violations mean a denied payout, which is final, and profit attributable to the activity may be removed from the account. Bungee may also apply a minimum holding time below which profit on a position is not credited, and clearing that floor is not a safe harbor.
  • Opposite exposure (long on one side, short on the other) across your own accounts, between traders, or against another exchange or prop firm is prohibited, including in different markets and at different sizes.
  • Copy trading, account sharing and latency or stale-price exploits are prohibited.

Profit Split and Payouts

The split is 80% trader / 20% Bungee, with no higher tier. Payouts are two separate steps:

  1. Claim profit. All eligible settled profit above the starting balance is removed, the 80% share is credited to your withdrawable balance, and the account resets to its original balance and default risk limits. It stays funded and does not need a new evaluation.
  2. Request payment. You request USDC from that balance within a shared allowance of $20,000 gross profit per user per UTC day, before the split. The minimum is $50 after the split, and Bungee targets processing within one business day, not as a guarantee.

KYC is required after you pass, before funded payouts. Payouts can be held for manual review where trading looks suspicious, thin-market profits are involved or compliance review applies, and a rejected request is final: it restores neither the balance nor the day's allowance. Per-trader caps are $300,000 of combined active challenge size and $110,000 of combined active funded size, counted separately.

Payout Verification

Bungee says it publishes a public payout ledger with the amount, time, chain and transaction hash of each payout, plus aggregate trust stats. We have not independently verified any individual payout or the ledger totals against the chain, so we do not quote them here and rate payouts as not yet independently verified.

Even a verified ledger would not prove solvency. We found no published reserve or proof of reserves, and the accounts behind the payouts are simulated records that Bungee can hedge or not at its discretion. We have not received a payout ourselves, and we do not track Bungee in our automated Basescan payout stats.

What Gives Us Pause

The program is young and the paperwork is moving. The rulebook is still labelled a draft at v0.10, ten versions in under three months, and the latest version added a new account type on 26 November. We could not find a Terms document written for the funded program or a published refund policy. There is no proof of reserves. Bots, APIs and scalping are banned and judged at Bungee's discretion without a published threshold, which rules out automated strategies and makes fast traders carry payout risk. Hedging rules reach across accounts, people and exchanges. Leverage is low on most markets (2x on most altcoins and most stocks), the hard breach is unforgiving, and the cheapest Turbo track leaves only 3% of room.

The Verdict

Bungee Funded Perps is a good fit for discretionary traders who want low entry cost, one step with no time pressure, very low fees. Payouts are not yet independently verified by us. A Turbo $10K at $45 or a Classic $10K at $90 is cheap to try. If you trade with bots, scalp, or need high leverage on altcoins, look elsewhere; Propr, for example, supports bots and AI agents. Because the program is new, the rulebook is still changing and no reserve is published, start with a small account and a small first claim, and re-read the current rules before you buy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bungee Funded Perps an onchain prop firm?

Only partly. Challenge and funded accounts are records in Bungee's own ledger, and the rulebook calls the Bungee dashboard and internal ledger the authoritative source of truth, so the rules are not enforced by smart contracts. What is onchain is the money: fees are paid in USDC and payouts are USDC transfers on Base, listed in a public payout ledger with transaction hashes.

How do Bungee Funded Perps payouts work?

Payouts are two steps. First you claim eligible profit from a funded account: the trader's 80% share is credited to your withdrawable balance and the account resets to its starting balance. Then you request payment in USDC, up to a shared allowance of $20,000 gross profit per user per UTC day. The minimum is $50 after the split, the target is one business day, and KYC is required before funded payouts.

What are the Bungee Funded Perps drawdown and daily loss rules?

Maximum drawdown is static: 6% on Classic, 5% on Pro and 3% on Turbo, measured from the starting balance. The daily loss limit is 3% of the settled balance at the 00:00 UTC reset, checked against live equity. Any breach is a hard breach with no reset or grace period, and you must buy a new challenge to continue.

Does Bungee Funded Perps allow bots, scalping or hedging?

No. The rulebook bans bots and API-driven trading, high-frequency trading and scalping on challenge and funded accounts, with no published holding-time threshold, and a confirmed violation means a denied payout. Opposite exposure across accounts, traders or external platforms is also prohibited, even in different markets.

How much does a Bungee Funded Perps challenge cost?

Fees are one-time and priced in USDC. A Turbo $10K starts at $45, a Pro $10K is $70 and a Classic $10K is $90. A $100K account costs $360 on Turbo, $600 on Pro and $900 on Classic. Prices can change, so check the live checkout page.
