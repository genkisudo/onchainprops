What Is Propr?

Propr is a perps prop firm built on Hyperliquid, a decentralized perpetual futures exchange. It is run by Propr Limited (British Virgin Islands, Reg. No. 2211330), backed by SwissBorg, and launched on 14 April 2026. Trader accounts are simulated: your trades are signals, and Propr decides whether to copy them to Hyperliquid. What Propr makes public is the firm side: every payout, its reserve wallets, its hedging account, revenue and pass rates are on its transparency page.

Propr lists 140+ crypto, stock, index, commodity and forex perps (Hyperliquid markets with more than $1M of open interest), all tradable 24/7 in one terminal. As of Nov 26, 2026, Propr's public stats show $4.82M paid to traders across 10,367 payouts.

Propr Profit Split

Funded traders keep 80% of all profits. The remaining 20% goes to Propr. The split is the same on every account size and challenge type, with no tiers or conditions attached.

Propr Payout Structure

Payouts are on demand, in USDC onchain, from a $20 minimum. There is no maximum, no limit on how many you request and no payout fee. Each payout sweeps all available profit (no partial withdrawals), you must close all open positions first, and the balance then resets to the starting amount.

The rulebook promises processing within 24 hours. In practice payout times vary day to day. Propr publishes them live, and every payout is listed with its transaction hash, on the transparency page.

Propr Trading Rules and Leverage

Risk limits: a max daily loss and a max drawdown apply at all times on both challenge and funded accounts, measured on equity (open positions count).

Profit targets: only during the challenge; funded accounts have none. 10% on Classic 1-Step, 9% on Turbo, 12% on Pro, and 5% then 10% on Classic 2-Step.

Permitted strategies: no restrictions on scalping, news trading, overnight or weekend holding, bots, AI agents, or copy trading (including between your own Propr accounts). Propr Fee Structure

Standard Hyperliquid maker/taker fees are passed through at cost with no markup, and Hyperliquid funding rates apply to open positions. There are no monthly, inactivity, data, platform or withdrawal fees.

Propr Maximum Daily Loss Rules

The maximum daily loss caps how much your equity can drop within a single day. It is a percentage of your start-of-day balance, recalculated every day at 00:00 UTC, so the dollar allowance shrinks as you draw down and grows as you bank profit.

1-Step accounts: 3% of start-of-day balance. 2-Step accounts: 5%. Example: start the day at $100,000 on a 2-Step account and your floor for the day is $95,000; draw down to $90,000 and the next day's limit is $4,500 (floor $85,500).

Propr Maximum Drawdown Rules

The maximum drawdown sets an absolute floor for the account: the lowest your equity can ever go. The calculation differs between 1-Step and 2-Step accounts.

1-Step accounts (static drawdown): the floor is your starting balance minus 6% (Classic), 3% (Turbo) or 5% (Pro). It is set once at account creation and never moves.

Example: on a $50,000 Classic 1-Step account the floor is $47,000. Grow the account to $60,000 and the floor is still $47,000. If equity touches $47,000 at any point, even intraday on an open position, the account is breached.

2-Step accounts (trailing drawdown): the floor is your high-water mark (highest equity, including open profit) minus 8% of the starting balance. It trails up with new highs but stops once it reaches the starting balance. A payout resets the high-water mark to the starting balance, so the floor drops back to its original level.

Example: on a $100,000 2-Step account the floor starts at $92,000. A peak of $105,000 lifts it to $97,000; a peak of $108,000 lifts it to $100,000, where it stops. After a payout it resets to $92,000.

Onchain Transparency: Payouts, Reserve and Hedging

Propr's accounts are simulated, so the rules are not onchain. What is onchain is the firm side: every payout settles in USDC with a public transaction hash, the reserve sits in public Hyperliquid margin, hot and cold wallets, and any hedge Propr takes runs on its own public Hyperliquid account. All of it is linked from propr.xyz/transparency.

Each trade on your dashboard is labelled A-booked (copied to Hyperliquid, visible onchain) or B-booked (recorded internally, not visible onchain). The P&L outcome is identical either way.

Propr's hedging wallet: you can follow the firm's hedging activity on Hyperliquid live at 0x3b9e…c503 on Hyperdash. Note: this is Propr's hedging wallet, not its payout wallet.

How Propr Decides Who Gets A-Booked: The Open-Source Algorithm

Propr's rulebook says the A/B decision is at the firm's discretion, trade by trade, weighing market conditions, the firm's total exposure, trade size and hedging needs. Propr has also published an open-source scoring algorithm under XBorgLabs/ab-booking on GitHub (@xborglabs/ab-booking on npm) that shows how it classifies traders.

Every trader starts on B-book. The algorithm runs continuously against your accumulated trade history and produces a composite score from 0 to 100. Routing is graduated — there is no hard switch. A score of ~70 routes roughly 50% of your order flow live. A score of 90 or above means 100% live A-book routing.

Five Scoring Dimensions

Dimension Weight What It Measures
Edge 30–40% Sharpe ratio, profit factor, and expectancy — adjusted for statistical confidence. A high Sharpe on only 15 trades is heavily discounted.
Behavior 25–30% Position sizing consistency, session regularity, and avoidance of high-volatility windows like US/London opens and funding rate resets.
Risk 20% Max drawdown relative to your challenge limit, recovery factor, and tail risk (CVaR, skewness).
Sample Confidence Multiplicative gate Trade count, time in market, and asset diversity. This multiplier gates the entire score — thin data keeps all scores low regardless of results.
Market-Relative 15–20% Whether returns are genuine alpha or just riding market direction. Checks for directional imbalance and profit concentration in a single trade.

Red and Green Flags

Independent of the composite score, the algorithm raises flags that can influence the final routing decision. A flagged account may be held on B-book for manual review even if the composite score is high enough for A-book classification.

Red flags (B-book indicators): high win rate with poor risk:reward ratio (martingale pattern), challenge completed in under 5 days, equity curve with no discernible trend, and drawdown close to the challenge limit.

Green flags (A-book support): Sharpe above 1.5 over 50+ trades, consistent performance across multiple challenge phases, drawdown well below limit, and profitable on 3 or more distinct assets.

Propr Country Availability

Propr is open to traders in most countries, including the US and UK. Accessing Propr from a restricted country, including via VPN, ends the account with no refund or payout.

Propr KYC Verification and Refund Policy

If your KYC verification is rejected, you will receive a notification with the reason. Common reasons include blurry documents, mismatched names, or unsupported document types.

All evaluation purchases are final and non-refundable. This applies whether you breach, fail to complete the evaluation, or choose not to trade. Review the full trading rules before purchasing to make sure the evaluation is right for you.
