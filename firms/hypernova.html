Hypernova is an on-chain prop trading protocol that pays out by smart contract in seconds, with every account record (rules, equity and payout history) anchored on-chain and publicly verifiable. It is currently in a closed, invite-only beta.

Hypernova's terminal, analytics and risk engine are built in-house. Prices, order book depth and funding rates come from live Hyperliquid market data, but orders are filled by Hypernova's own engine on simulated accounts and are not routed to Hyperliquid. The catalogue covers 100+ perpetual markets across crypto, equities, commodities and pre-IPO names. When you buy an assessment you pick a Crypto, TradFi or All Markets package.

Hypernova's public stats page shows $3.75M in lifetime payouts to 432 traders and 641 funded accounts (as of Nov 26, 2026).

Profit Split: 80%, or 90% with the Add-On

Every funded account keeps 80% of profits by default. Hypernova also sells a 90% profit split add-on: tick it when you buy a Tight, Low or Medium risk assessment and you pay 20% more than the standard fee (a $275 Low risk $25K assessment becomes $330). The split is written on-chain at purchase and applies to every payout from that funded account. High risk accounts stay at 80%.

Risk Parameters

All assessments are 1-step. You choose an account size from $5K to $200K and one of four risk tiers, which set the profit target and how much drawdown room you get.

The max drawdown is a static floor set from your starting balance; it never trails. The daily loss limit resets at 00:00 UTC from the prior day's closing balance. Both limits are enforced on equity, so a floating loss can breach the account. Leverage is capped per symbol (see Hypernova's Markets page). There is no time limit, no minimum trading days and no consistency rule. Hypernova publishes pass rates on its pricing page: 18.7% for Tight, 20.5% for Low and 30.5% for Medium.

You can hold up to $300,000 in combined starting capital across active assessments and funded accounts, with at most two assessments running at once. A Fast Track option lets traders connect their Hyperliquid, Binance or Bybit history for a score; qualifying traders can skip the assessment.

Payouts

Once your identity is verified and your funded account is flat (no open positions, orders or TWAPs), you enter an amount on the Payouts page and sign once. The smart contract releases your share as USDC to your embedded wallet on Arbitrum, with gas paid by Hypernova. There is no manual approval and no minimum. Hypernova's docs quote about 5 seconds end to end; its live stats show a 6.1-second average. Traditional prop firms typically take 24+ hours.

Limits to know: during the private beta, payouts are capped at $10,000 of profit per user per UTC day across all funded accounts. After each payout you must open and close a position before the next one. Payouts are also unavailable for a short time after your first assessment purchase and while an account is under compliance or risk review, and Hypernova may pause them for security or network reasons.

Smart Contract Transparency

Hypernova records your entire trading lifecycle on-chain. Your assessment account, funded account, the rules you trade under, the agreement you sign, and every payout you take are written to public smart contracts on Arbitrum that anyone can audit.

Hypernova operates the trading, risk, and settlement systems — but every account action is anchored on-chain, so you can independently verify your rules, equity, and payouts instead of relying on the firm's word alone.

Every account: Both assessment and funded accounts are created as records on the smart contract. There is no hidden ledger.

Every rule: Drawdown limits, profit targets, daily loss caps, and starting balance are written into the contract when your account is created.

Every signature: Accepting your funded-trader agreement produces a cryptographic signature that is verified on-chain before your account goes active.

Every payout: Profit splits, transaction hashes, and vault balances are published to the chain in real time and queryable by anyone.

Active Addresses

Hypernova runs on two smart contracts, backed by one Hypernova-controlled reserve wallet. All three live on Arbitrum One and can be inspected on Arbiscan at any time.

These smart contracts enforce the rules in code. Anyone can read them, and Hypernova cannot alter your recorded rules or release more than your earned profit off-chain.

Funding & Backing

Hypernova closed a $3 million pre-seed round led by Lemniscap, with participation from Very Early Ventures, CMS Holdings, and Pivot Global. Angel investors from across the Hyperliquid ecosystem also joined — including Maximilian Fiege (co-founder of Native Markets), "Ericonomic" of Kinetiq, "Huf" of Pear Protocol, and Kirby Ong, Noel Tan, and "Velocity," co-founders of HypurrCollective.

The HypurrCollective co-founders participated through their group on Echo, the on-chain capital-raising platform founded by Jordan "Cobie" Fish and later acquired by Coinbase for $375 million.

Team

Hypernova plans to add a quantitative researcher and developer to the team in the near term.
