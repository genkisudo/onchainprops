Before you pay for a challenge, ask one question: are onchain prop firms legit, and can I prove it before I pay? Profit split and account size are easy to compare. Whether the firm actually pays out is the part that costs traders money, and onchain it is something you can check.

## Are Onchain Prop Firms Legit?

Some are, and some aren't. "Onchain prop firm" is a loose label. It covers firms whose rules and payouts live in smart contracts, and firms that trade on a blockchain venue like Hyperliquid but run everything else on their own servers.

The label alone proves nothing. What matters is how much of the firm you can verify without trusting it. A firm that shows its payouts, its treasury and its rules publicly asks for less trust than one that shows a nice dashboard.

## Why Payout Verification Matters More Than Profit Split

A 90% split means nothing if the payout never arrives. The failure mode in prop trading is rarely a bad split. It is a firm that reviews your payout request, finds a reason to reject it, or pauses operations after collecting fees.

The problem is structural: in most prop firms, the company that took your fee also decides whether your payout is valid. Onchain, parts of that decision can move into public code and public transactions, which is why I rank payout proof above every other column in the table.

## 6-Step Checklist: How to Verify an Onchain Prop Firm

1. **Find real payouts with transaction hashes.** Ask for payout proof you can open on a block explorer. A screenshot of a payout is not proof. A transaction hash is. Payouts sent in stablecoins to wallets leave a public trail.
2. **Check the treasury or reserve.** A visible onchain wallet or reserve shows whether there is money behind the promises. Compare its size to the payouts the firm advertises.
3. **See where the rules live.** If payout eligibility is enforced by a smart contract, no one has to approve it by hand. If rules sit in the firm's database, you are trusting the firm to apply them fairly.
4. **Know which dashboard you're reading.** Onchain dashboards read from smart contracts and blockchain transactions. Offchain dashboards pull from the firm's own database, so you are taking its word for it.
5. **Check how the firm makes money.** Does it earn when you win, or when you fail? A firm that lives on evaluation fees and B-books its traders does not need you to succeed. See [how prop firms make money](how-do-prop-firms-make-money) for the A-book vs B-book breakdown.
6. **Look at the team and the entity.** Check whether the founders are named, and where the company is registered. An anonymous team with no entity gives you nowhere to go if a payout is refused.

No firm passes all six perfectly, and nothing guarantees safety. The goal is to know exactly what you are trusting before you pay.

## Red Flags to Watch For

- **Payout "proof" without transaction hashes.** Screenshots and testimonials are easy to fake.
- **A payout ledger you can't cross-check.** If entries can't be matched to a chain, they are the firm's claims, not data.
- **No visible treasury.** You can't tell if funded traders can actually be paid.
- **Vague account type.** The firm calls an account "funded" but won't say whether your trades are simulated or hedged.
- **Rules that change after you pay.** Look for versioned, public rules rather than a page that can be edited quietly.
- **Pressure to buy now.** Countdown timers and "last discount" banners do not tell you anything about solvency.

## A Simple Pre-Purchase Routine

Before paying for any challenge:

1. Open the firm's payout page and pick three recent payouts.
2. Look each one up on a block explorer and confirm the amount, the date and the receiving wallet.
3. Find the treasury or reserve and see whether it can cover what has been paid.
4. Read the rules for payout approval and note who decides.
5. Start with the smallest account size. Keep your first fee small until the firm has paid you.

It takes about ten minutes, and it is the cheapest risk control a prop trader has.

## FAQ

### Are onchain prop firms legit?

Some are, some aren't. Verify payouts with transaction hashes, check the treasury, and see whether rules are enforced by smart contracts before you buy.

### How can I verify an onchain prop firm's payouts?

Ask for payout transaction hashes and look them up on a block explorer. Confirm the amount, date and receiving wallet. Screenshots are not proof.

### Are onchain prop firm funded accounts real?

Often they are simulated, with real payouts on top. Ask whether your account is hedged in the market, and look for the hedges on a public venue like Hyperliquid.

### What is the biggest red flag in a prop firm?

Payout "proof" you can't check. If you can't match a payout to a blockchain transaction, you are relying on the firm's word.

### Is a high profit split a sign of a good prop firm?

No. A high split only matters if payouts arrive. Check payout proof first, then compare splits.
