What Is Vanta Trading?

Vanta Trading is a prop trading evaluation platform built on Bittensor Subnet 8 (Vanta Network). You pay a one-time fee, pass a one-step challenge with a 10% profit target on a simulated account, and earn weekly rewards in USDC or by bank transfer, with a 100% profit split by default.

How Do Crypto Prop Firms Make Money? A-Book vs B-Book Explained

Prop firms make money from evaluation fees, their share of the profit split on funded accounts, and extras like resets. On a B-book, traders sit on simulated accounts and the firm takes the other side, so trader losses are firm revenue. On an A-book, trader flow is hedged in the market and the firm earns when traders do.

Polymarket Prop Firms: Funded Prediction Market Trading Compared

Polymarket prop firms sell a one-time evaluation on simulated event-contract accounts priced off live Polymarket order books, then pay 70–90% of profits on funded accounts. Live today are Funding Predicts, PolyFundr, PropMarket, FundMyPrediction and Cashed. None publishes a treasury or payout wallet, so onchain verification is limited to your own payout transfer.

What Is Hyperliquid?

Hyperliquid is a layer-1 blockchain built around an onchain exchange. Its core, HyperCore, runs perpetual futures and spot order books with one-block finality, and HyperEVM adds smart contracts on the same chain. HIP-3 lets anyone deploy perp markets, builder codes let apps earn fees on the trades they route, and HIP-4 adds outcome markets.
